Desk Calendar for KOReader, custom build for personal use, 2026.

The plugin UI, model integration and sample content were authored for this project.
The lunar.lua component is a Lua adaptation of Ho Ngoc Duc's lunar calendar
algorithm, based on astronomical formulae from Jean Meeus (1998).
